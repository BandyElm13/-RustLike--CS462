RUSTLIKE
A first-person, wave-based PvE roguelike by BREWSLABS, built for CS462 in Unity.

Fight increasingly difficult enemy waves, upgrade your weapons, and escape to the next planet to keep your run alive.

The Game
The design document describes the intended experience below. These are design goals, not a checklist of completed features.

Core Loop
Begin each run on the same starting planet.
Fight waves of planet-themed enemies and collect upgrades and abilities.
Explore optional procedurally generated dungeon areas for stronger loot—and tougher enemies.
Escape by spaceship when the planet becomes overwhelming, then face a harder planet.
Discover every planet, or keep playing to chase your longest survival time.
Death ends the run. Research components carry over between runs and can be used to upgrade weapons.

Design Highlights
Fast movement and combat: inspired by Quake and Megabonk.
Planet-based encounters: robot, alien, and rock-themed worlds, with gravity pulling toward each planet's center.
Weapons and abilities: upgradeable firearms, melee attacks, and abilities that boost movement, fire rate, or survivability.
Light-hearted sci-fi: low-poly, cartoon-inspired visuals with a comedic, dystopian feel.
Readable presentation: a simple HUD and energetic, cartoon-style audio.
